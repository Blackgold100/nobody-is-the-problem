## About the Author

Lincoln Matthew is the Programme Manager of Zeeb Healthcare Development Initiative (ZHDI), a non-governmental organization in Nigeria working to improve health outcomes and accountability in public services. A graduate of Medical Laboratory Science from Niger Delta University (B.MLS, 2023), he began university studies in Computer Science at Benson Idahosa University before circumstances forced a change of course. Much of the author’s adult life has been spent navigating the same institutions this book examines—from the education system to the health system—most painfully while caring for a mother through chronic kidney disease until her death in 2022. Nobody Is the Problem grew out of those experiences and out of a question the author kept asking without finding an answer: why do hardworking Nigerians keep meeting the same disappointing outcomes, whoever is in charge? He lives and works in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State. Readers can reach him through the book’s website.
